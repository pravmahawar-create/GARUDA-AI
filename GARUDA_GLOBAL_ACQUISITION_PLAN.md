## 1. EXECUTIVE ACQUISITION AUDIT

GARUDA currently possesses **scaffolded discovery modules and one live public job feed**:
- **Current Feeds:** Polls Remotive API every 15 minutes (`opportunityDiscoveryService.js`).
- **Current Outbound Channel:** `emailRelayService.js` (HTTP API relay for Brevo/Resend/SendGrid) and `outboundCommunicationService.js` (gated behind Founder approval).
- **Current Inbound Channel:** Public chat widget (`garudaos.in/chat`) and Telegram bot (`telegramBotService.js`).
- **Current Acquisition Limitation:** The system captures remote employment listings rather than commercial agency/software development contracts. It lacks an inbound commercial intake funnel and automated outbound sending loops.

## 2. STRICT ETHICAL & COMPLIANCE BOUNDARIES

GARUDA will **NOT** rely on:
- ❌ Prohibited web scraping of platforms that explicitly disallow automated access (e.g. scraping LinkedIn without API licenses).
- ❌ Mass unsolicited cold spam or email bombing (which destroys domain deliverability and violates CAN-SPAM / GDPR).
- ❌ Deceptive personas, fake bot identities, or unverified claims.

All acquisition strategies must operate within:
- ✅ Official public APIs, authorized RSS project feeds, and open developer bounty protocols.
- ✅ Inbound search traffic, organic discoverability, and high-converting commercial intake wizards.
- ✅ Verified B2B outreach with transparent identity attribution: *"Praveen Mahawar & GARUDA AI Engineering"*.

### Pillar 1: Inbound Commercial Funnel on `garudaos.in`
1. **Interactive Scoping Wizard:** Replace the generic "Get Started" sign-up modal with an **Interactive Project Scoping Wizard**:
   - Deliverable Type (Web App, SaaS MVP, AI Assistant, Workflow Automation, API Integration).
   - Tech Stack & Complexity (React/Node.js, Python, Supabase, Payment Gateway).
   - Instant Automated Scope & Fixed Price Estimate (e.g. "$2,500 USD — 7 Business Days").
2. **Immediate Deposit Checkout:** Client reviews generated milestone package and pays a 30% deposit via integrated Razorpay/Stripe checkout.
3. **Automated Mission Initialization:** Verified payment webhook triggers instant creation of a `RevenueExecutionMission` in Mission Control.

### Pillar 2: Legitimate Public Project Feeds & Bounty Aggregators
1. **Authorized Project RSS Feeds:** Ingest public, authorized RSS project feeds from freelance networks and open freelance job boards (e.g. RemoteOK, WeWorkRemotely, specialized contractor boards).
2. **Open Source Bounties & Ecosystem Grants:** Crawl and evaluate open developer bounties (GitHub Issue Bounties, Algorand/Ethereum developer grant RFPs, AI hackathon commercial tracks).
3. **Algorithmic Opportunity Scoring:** Score listings against GARUDA’s verified 12-category capability registry (`capabilityRegistryService.js`) and generate bespoke milestone solutions.

### Pillar 3: Compliance-First Outbound Commercial Relay
1. **Verified Business Target Research:** Target verified public B2B business inquiries, agency RFP pages, and businesses publicly requesting software development.
2. **Tailored Proposal Synthesis:** Use `revenueAcquisitionService.js` to compile grounded, evidence-backed technical proposals highlighting exact architecture, milestone timelines, and fixed costs.
3. **Founder-in-the-Loop 1-Click Dispatch:** Proposal packages are pushed to Founder Telegram with inline buttons (`[Send Email]`, `[Edit]`, `[Dismiss]`). One tap dispatches the email via configured Brevo/Resend relay.

### Pillar 4: Vertical Coexistence (Insurance & Education)
1. **Vertical Isolation:** The ABSLI life insurance advisory engine (`telegramInsuranceWorkerService.js`, `abslKnowledgeService.js`) and tutoring lead scanner (`tutoringLeadScoutService.js`) run as **dedicated vertical sub-universes**.
2. **Platform Separation:** The public face of `garudaos.in` is strictly positioned as a **Global Custom AI & Software Engineering Company**, while insurance and tutoring operate through dedicated outbound lead pipelines and local Telegram bots.

## 4. CONVERSION INTELLIGENCE & LEAD SCORING PIPELINE

Every prospect passing through GARUDA is evaluated across 5 deterministic criteria:

```text
PROSPECT INTAKE ──► INTENT & CAPABILITY MATCH ──► BUDGET & RISK SCORING ──► PROPOSAL SYNTHESIS ──► FOUNDER NOTIFICATION
```

| Evaluation Factor | Scoring Criteria | Action Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Capability Alignment** | Match against verified skills (Node.js, React, APIs, RAG, Automation). | Score ≥ 75/100 to proceed. |
| **Budget Feasibility** | Stated or estimated budget ≥ minimum floor ($250 USD / ₹20,000 INR). | Below floor = Auto-rejected. |
| **Scam & Prohibited Filter** | Rejects gambling, adult content, pay-upfront schemes, and unverified contacts. | Immediate rejection if flagged. |
| **Urgency & Intent** | Freshness of request (< 48 hours) and clarity of requirements. | Priority bonus (+15 points). |
