Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I avoid Bank Relationship Managers (RMs) for term insurance?
Bank RMs operate under heavy quarterly sales quotas to sell expensive ULIPs and endowment products with high surrender penalties. An independent advisor focuses strictly on pure term coverage with proper riders and claim assurance under IRDAI Section 45.
What is the difference between Health Insurance (Mediclaim) and Critical Illness Rider?
Health insurance (Mediclaim) only reimburses actual hospital room and doctor bills after deductibles. A Critical Illness rider pays out a 100% full lump-sum cash amount directly to your bank account immediately upon diagnosis of any of the 64 covered major ailments, providing income replacement during recovery.
What happens if I stop paying premiums in DigiShield TROP?
After paying premiums for a minimum of 2-3 consecutive years, the policy acquires a guaranteed surrender and paid-up value, protecting your capital from complete forfeiture.
