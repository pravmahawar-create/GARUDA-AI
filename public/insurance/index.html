Legal clarity

Understand Section 45

Section 45 of the Insurance Act sets statutory limits on when a life policy may be called into question. It is not a promise of zero claim rejection. Fraud, material misstatement and statutory conditions matter.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear answers before you decide.

Is GARUDA OS the insurance company?

No. GARUDA OS is the advisory desk identity. The insurer is Aditya Birla Sun Life Insurance Company Limited. Policy issuance, underwriting, servicing decisions and claim decisions remain with ABSLI.

Can I get an exact premium on this website?

No. Exact premiums require official insurer calculation and underwriting. Use the ABSLI calculator and request an official benefit illustration.

Are all products listed always available?

Not necessarily. Product availability and versions can change. Confirm the current product page, brochure and UIN with ABSLI before solicitation.

Are tax benefits guaranteed?

No. Tax treatment depends on current tax law, policy issue date, premium-to-sum-assured conditions and other statutory requirements. Seek qualified tax advice.
