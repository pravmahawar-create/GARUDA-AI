# GARUDA — The 27 Foundational Universes

## Purpose and authority

This document is the permanent canonical architecture constitution for GARUDA's
Foundational Universes. It defines the exactly **27 Universes** that together form
one GARUDA, their relationships, the cross-cutting Global Layers they share, and
the governed loops through which GARUDA evolves, heals itself, and engineers itself.

- **Authority order:** GARUDA Constitution > explicit founder-locked decisions > this document > current architecture > roadmap > historical reports.
- **One Command. Infinite Intelligence.** GARUDA is an AI Operating System and AI
  Earning Partner. It converts a single founder command into governed planning,
  execution, validation, learning, and measurable outcomes — while preserving
  founder sovereignty.
- This document does not create a new Universe, rename a Universe, or delete one.
  The 27 names below are final and canonical.

## Status legend

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `EXISTING` | Operational today in the repository/runtime; verifiable by running code. |
| `PARTIAL` | Real subsystems exist and work, but the full Universe scope is not yet delivered. |
| `ARCHITECTED` | Founder-locked or fully designed; implementation is next, not yet operational. |
| `FUTURE` | Roadmap Universe; identity and mission are fixed, scope is to be built. |

## Rule #1 — Preservation

1. **Exactly 27 canonical Universes.** No new Universe may be invented, no name
   renamed, no Universe duplicated, and none removed — regardless of how tempting
   a launch slice looks.
2. **Specialization without isolation.** Universes are specialized, never walled
   off. Any Universe may receive intelligence, memory, creativity, or execution
   support from any other Universe. Artificial capability walls are prohibited.
3. **The Brain is not a Universe.** Mother Brain, the Brain Capability, Brain-to-
   Brain, and Collective Intelligence are cross-cutting architecture — not a 28th
   Universe. They are Global Layers (see below).
4. **No "Language Universe."** Language is a Global Layer served by the
   Communication Universe, Memory, and the Multilingual Layer — not a Universe.
5. **No "Holographic Universe."** Presence evolves text → voice → avatar → spatial
   through the Digital Presence Universe and the Presence Global Layer — not a
   Universe.
6. **History is preserved, not rewritten.** Where older documents conflict with
   this constitution, the conflict is recorded here and resolved forward. Nothing
   is silently deleted.

## GARUDA as an AI Operating System

GARUDA is one mind expressed through specialized surfaces. A single natural-language
goal enters Mother Brain, is sensed against the world and against GARUDA's own
intelligence, decomposed into governed work, executed by specialized Universes and
workers, validated, reported, learned from, and used to evolve GARUDA itself.

The A-I promise: **A**mbition (the founder's goal) is converted to **I**ntelligence
(deployed capability) through the Four Rings below. Every ring, every Universe, is
part of one GARUDA — no Universe is a silo, a product, or a separate company.

## The One-GARUDA principles

- **Specialization without isolation.** Depth in every Universe; reach everywhere.
- **Founder sovereignty.** The founder commands, approves, and owns. No Universe
  acts outside governed authority.
- **Governed autonomy.** GARUDA runs itself inside the Constitution and the
  Approval Gate, never around them.
- **Truth over comfort.** GARUDA never claims capability it lacks (Amendment 7).
  Statuses here are honest.
- **Reuse before build.** Existing engines (Knowledge, RAG, Semantic Ranker,
  Revenue, Brain components, Workforce Router, Scheduler, settlement truth-chain)
  are shared, never duplicated.
- **Evolution by loop, not by accident.** Change flows through the Evolution,
  Self-Healing, and Self-Engineering loops — all governed.

# RING 1 — CORE INTELLIGENCE (Universes 1–9)

### Universe 1 — Knowledge
**Status:** PARTIAL · **Scope:** founder · **Ring:** Core Intelligence

- **Mission:** Acquire, organize, and deliver governed knowledge — GARUDA's
  own engineering and business knowledge plus the world's — on demand.
- **Vision:** A living library that answers faster than it is asked and becomes
  smarter every time it is used.
- **Intelligence:** Retrieval, synthesis, and ranking intelligence over trusted
  sources, blending repository truth with world knowledge.
- **Capabilities:** Semantic search, RAG-based grounded answers, category-aware
  knowledge retrieval, engineering/business knowledge retrieval, knowledge products.
- **Inputs:** Founder questions, documents, repository code, external sources
  permitted by governance, memory from other Universes.
- **Outputs:** Grounded answers, citations, knowledge packets, training material,
  decision briefs for Mother Brain and other Universes.
- **Internal Systems:** Knowledge Engine, RAG Engine, Semantic Ranker,
  `knowledgeService` (search, category search), ABSL knowledge services,
  knowledge seed services.
- **Cross/Outbound Enhancement:** Supplies grounded context to Reasoning,
  Communication, Revenue, Business, Education, Health, Creative, and every other
  Universe that asks.
- **Mother Brain Relationship:** Knowledge is Mother Brain's fact-checking hand;
  answers are validated against it before high-stakes use.
- **Human Interaction:** Founder asks in natural language; answers are readable,
  honest, and source-linked.
- **Autonomous Operation:** Retrieval and synthesis run autonomously; publishing
  or external sourcing requires governance.
- **Learning Loop:** Usage, gaps, and correction feedback feed back into ranking
  and source quality.
- **Self-Improvement:** Reweighs sources, prunes stale knowledge, closes gaps
  flagged by unanswered or corrected questions.
- **Revenue/Value Potential:** Knowledge products, premium briefs, and advisory
  content feed the Revenue Universe.
- **Security & Governance:** External sources pass Security; knowledge touching
  private/business data is gated by governance and never leaks to the repository.
- **Dependencies:** Memory (context), Reasoning (synthesis), Security, Governance,
  RAG infra, MongoDB.
- **Completion Definition:** Any query returns a grounded, honest answer — or an
  explicit admission that the answer is not known.
- **Future Expansion:** Live continuous ingestion, cross-lingual knowledge, and
  self-maintained source graphs.

# The Evolution Loop

GARUDA grows in governed cycles. Each cycle is ten steps:

1. **WORLD** — Continuous World Intelligence observes real-world signals.
2. **SENSE** — Security and Memory filter what matters and what is safe.
3. **ASSIMILATE** — Knowledge and Learning absorb and organize the new.
4. **CONNECT** — Collective Intelligence and the Cross-Universe Network relate it
   to existing capability.
5. **BUILD** — Reasoning and Automation turn insight into new capability.
6. **DEPLOY** — The Governed Self-Engineering Loop ships it safely.
7. **LEARN** — Validator and Learning measure the outcome.
8. **EVOLVE** — Self-Evolution updates prompts, configs, and weights.
9. **SELF-IMPROVE** — Continuous Intelligence Building compounds the gain.
10. **EXPAND** — The founder sees a stronger GARUDA, and the loop repeats.

Every cycle is auditable, reversible, and founder-visible. Evolution is governed
growth, never silent mutation.

# The Self-Healing Loop

GARUDA repairs itself before problems compound:

1. **DETECT** — Security, Validator, and Reporter flag a deviation.
2. **DIAGNOSE** — Reasoning and Memory isolate the cause and impact.
3. **PLAN** — ArchitectBrain proposes a bounded repair.
4. **APPROVE** — The Founder Approval Gate admits the repair (bounded fixes may
   run on standing authority; core changes need founder approval).
5. **HEAL** — Controlled Patch Application applies the repair.
6. **VERIFY** — Validator proves the system is whole again.
7. **RECORD** — The event enters Memory and the audit trail.
8. **PREVENT** — Learning converts the incident into a permanent guard.

Healing is honest: GARUDA reports what broke and what it did about it.

# The Governed Self-Engineering Loop

GARUDA engineers itself — but only through a governed gate that protects the
founder's sovereignty and the repository. This is the realized form of the
Evolution and Self-Healing loops when the change is to GARUDA's own code.

1. **Natural Language Goal** — the founder (or Mother Brain, with authority)
   states what GARUDA should do.
2. **ArchitectBrain** — turns the goal into a bounded engineering plan:
   scope, affected files, tests, and risk.
3. **EngineeringBrain** — executes the build against the plan inside the
   `GovernedEngineeringLoop`, using the `GenericCodeTaskEngine` and governed
   patch policies (`EngineeringProposalPolicy`).
4. **LLM Proposal** — the LLM produces a concrete patch with a SHA256
   fingerprint and a new-files-only guard where applicable.
5. **ReviewerBrain** — reviews the proposal: correctness, security, constitution
   alignment, and test coverage.
6. **Founder Approval Gate** — the founder approves or rejects. No gate bypass.
7. **Controlled Patch Application** — the approved patch is applied to the
   workspace (`EngineeringBrain.applyPatchToWorkspace`, executor `patch` route).
8. **Verification** — build, tests, and module load are proven
   (`READ/WRITE_PATCH/DIFF_INSPECTION/TEST_EXECUTION` capabilities).
9. **Completion / Rollback** — on success the loop reports completion; on
   failure it rolls back and records the lesson.
10. **Learning** — the outcome feeds the Learning Universe for the next loop.

Today this path is real: the patch-apply route is verified end-to-end
(build → patch → apply → verify → module load); the full loop is prototype-stage
and never touches the repository without the founder's approval. That honesty is
part of the Constitution.
