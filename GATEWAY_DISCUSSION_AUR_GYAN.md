## 3. THE 2.5% MYTH: SOFTWARE CODE SE INTERCHANGE KYU NAHI HAT-TA
Client ko samjhana hai ki card processing ka paisa kahan jaata hai:
1. **Interchange Fee (1.5% - 2.0%)**: Card issue karne wale bank ko jaati hai (e.g. Chase, Barclays, HDFC).
2. **Card Network Scheme Fee (0.15% - 0.3%)**: Visa aur Mastercard company ko jaati hai.
3. **Acquiring Bank Fee (0.3% - 0.5%)**: Processing bank ko jaati hai.
4. **Gateway / Software Markup (0.3% - 0.5%)**: Stripe / Razorpay ka software profit.

Agar koi coder bole ki *"Mai code likhkar card processing 0% kar dunga"*, to wo jhooth bol raha hai. Software sirf **0.3% software margin** bacha sakta hai. 
**Asli bachat tab hoti hai jab aap customer ko high-cost card se hata kar low-cost direct rails par shift karwate ho!**

## 7. SWIFT KE 15 DIN VS LOCAL RAILS KE 10 MINUTE (SETTLEMENT SECRETS)
**Client Ka Doubt**: *"SWIFT to 10-15 din leta hai, tum turant kaise settle karoge?"*

**Kyu SWIFT Slow Hai?**:
- Purana SWIFT route: Sender Bank ➔ Intermediary Bank 1 ➔ Intermediary Bank 2 ➔ Recipient Bank (har jagah manual inspection aur clearing hoti hai).

**Fintech Fast Rails (Local Clearing)**:
- **USA**: Domestic **ACH / Fedwire** rail use hoti hai (Settles in 10-60 minutes).
- **UK**: **Faster Payments** rail use hoti hai (Settles in 15 seconds).
- **Europe**: **SEPA Instant** rail use hoti hai (Settles in 5 seconds).
- **Card / Apple Pay**: **Visa Direct / Mastercard Send** rail (Settles T+0 same day ya T+1).
- **Instant Webhook Notification**: Hamara software 1-second me hotel/broker screen par "Payment Confirmed" badge dikha deta hai taaki deal turant lock ho sake.
