# GARUDA Billing — Support & Data Backup Guide

## 1. How Local Data Works
GARUDA Billing is built around the **Sovereign Local-First Doctrine**:
- Your business records (customers, invoices, payments, stock) live inside your phone's secure private storage.
- No third party, server, or cloud company has access to your customer lists or billing figures.

---

## 2. Recommended Backup Routine (Weekly or Daily)
Because your data is on your phone, taking regular backups ensures you never lose a single bill even if your phone is lost, damaged, or upgraded.

### How to Take a Backup (Takes 5 Seconds):
1. Open **GARUDA Billing**.
2. Tap on the **Settings** tab.
3. Tap **"Backup Data"** (or Export All).
4. A file named `garuda-billing-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` will be saved in your Downloads.
5. **Best Practice:** Share this JSON file to your own personal WhatsApp or email.

---

## 3. How to Restore on a New Phone
1. Install `GARUDA_Billing_v1.11_Release.apk` on your new phone.
2. Open Settings -> Tap **"Import / Restore Data"**.
3. Select your latest `garuda-billing-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` file.
4. All your customers, products, stock counts, and past invoices will restore instantly.
