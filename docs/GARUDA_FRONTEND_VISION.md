# GARUDA Frontend and Experience Vision

## Status

Founder-locked experience direction. The current GARUDA-AI frontend and Emergent Revenue dashboard are operational prototypes, not the final visual authority.

## Experience identity

The final product is a premium living Kingdom Portal and AI Operating System. It should feel cinematic, calm, powerful and coherent—not like a generic admin template or a collection of unrelated dashboards.

## Locked arrival sequence

1. Pure black entry
2. Wind and subtle particles
3. Lightning reveal
4. Official GARUDA sigil
5. Kingdom Portal
6. “Welcome to the World of GARUDA”
7. Founder verification
8. Enter GARUDA OS
9. Unified command dashboard

## Kingdom environment

- Mountains, waterfall, river and green forest
- Clouds, mist, dynamic sky and controlled particles
- Flying GARUDA/eagle presence
- Golden portal and premium black/gold identity
- Ambient wind, restrained sound and dynamic lighting
- Motion must communicate system state, not distract from work
- Performance, accessibility and reduced-motion support are mandatory

## GARUDA OS

- Mother is the single conversational and command entry point
- Universal Conversation Box / Founder Companion remains persistent
- One Command. Infinite Intelligence.
- Founder interaction is minimized toward Goal, Approve and Reject
- Mother Core visualization and live system-state panels
- Universe portals with consistent Brain, Memory, Dashboard and Actions contracts
- Thinking, planning, building, validating and learning states are visibly distinguishable
- Live logs are summarized for humans; raw diagnostic detail remains available on demand

## Mobile-first control room

Mobile is the primary command, approval and monitoring surface. It must provide:

- Mission creation and status
- Approval inbox with risk, cost and consequence preview
- Opportunity review and source evidence
- Active work, blockers and next actions
- Revenue, payment verification and Settlement Ledger
- Notifications and escalation controls
- Emergency pause and revoke controls
- No secret or privileged approval token exposed to the browser

## Prototype relationship

The Emergent repository remains useful as an authenticated Revenue workspace and integration prototype. Its current layout, sample opportunity pipeline and visual language must not silently replace the Kingdom/Experience lock. Components may be reused after architectural and visual review.

## Frontend authority boundary

The frontend is an adapter, not the authoritative runtime. It may request or display decisions, but Mother, Constitution, approval enforcement, audit records, revenue truth and settlement truth stay server-side.
