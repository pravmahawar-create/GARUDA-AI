# Mother Brain

## Overview

Mother Brain is the executive intelligence layer of GARUDA. It coordinates the full operating loop from founder intent through execution, validation, and improvement.

## Operating Loop

Founder Goal
↓
Think
↓
Plan
↓
Execute
↓
Validate
↓
Improve
↓
Founder Approval
↓
Earn

## Functional Roles

### Think
The system gathers context, identifies the strategic objective, and frames priorities clearly.

### Plan
GARUDA decomposes the objective into manageable actions and assigns the right resources, tools, and sequencing.

### Execute
Work is carried out through internal agents, integrated systems, and operational workflows.

### Validate
Outputs are checked against quality, relevance, and strategic alignment requirements.

### Improve
Feedback loops refine future performance through measured outcomes and accumulated memory.

### Founder Approval
High-impact decisions remain subject to founder review and authorization.

### Earn
The system converts completed work into commercial value, growth, or strategic advantage.

## Architecture Intent

Mother Brain acts as the central coordination mechanism for intelligence, execution, and value creation. It ensures that GARUDA remains operationally coherent rather than merely reactive.
