# AI Workforce

## Overview

GARUDA's future operating model includes a distributed workforce of specialized agents. These workers are designed to collaborate across strategy, execution, creation, growth, and commercialization.

## Worker Roles

### Software Engineer
Coordinates implementation, system integration, product logic, and technical execution.

### Research Agent
Investigates markets, technology, opportunities, and external signals to guide smarter decisions.

### Marketing Agent
Develops positioning, audience strategy, campaign architecture, messaging, and channel direction.

### Creator Agent
Supports the development of content, storytelling, visual output, and creative direction.

### Business Agent
Focuses on commercial structure, operations, partnerships, and strategic business planning.

### Automation Agent
Handles repetitive workflows, system coordination, task orchestration, and operational consistency.

### Revenue Agent
Optimizes monetization pathways, growth opportunities, and financial leverage across the platform.

## Coordination Model

The workforce operates as a network of specialized intelligence units. Each agent contributes domain expertise while remaining connected to shared planning, memory, and approval systems.
