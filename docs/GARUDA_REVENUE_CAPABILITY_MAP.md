# GARUDA Revenue Capability Map

## Locked commercial principle

GARUDA earns from GARUDA's authorized technical capabilities. A founder's personal eligibility is not the primary earning dependency. Human job feeds such as Remotive are secondary opportunity channels and must not define the autonomous earning architecture.

The income target is an optimization minimum, not a ceiling or guarantee. Discovery continues beyond the target. During an opportunity gap GARUDA rediscoveries, improves capabilities, productizes reusable work and reports honestly; it does not fabricate revenue or remain idle.

|---|---|---|
| Engineering | Software, integrations, automation, maintenance, reusable tools | Match demand, scope, price, verify delivery |

| Companion | Founder communication and continuity | Indirect enablement; not forced monetization |
| Guardian/Security | Safety, fraud prevention and risk reduction | Protect revenue; safety is never weakened for earnings |

Not every Universe must directly earn. Safety, memory, experience and orchestration can protect or multiply value without becoming standalone offers.

## Required end-to-end pipeline

1. Capability Registry declares what GARUDA can currently deliver and with what evidence.
2. Opportunity Discovery finds lawful demand from multiple authorized sources.
3. Capability Matcher rejects work GARUDA cannot honestly deliver.
4. Revenue Intelligence scores value, cost, risk, time and strategic reuse.
5. Mother creates an execution plan and approval checkpoints.
6. Universes produce the deliverable through the AI Workforce.
7. Validator and Guardian verify quality, policy, originality and security.
8. Founder approves external, paid, contractual or sensitive action.
9. Authorized founder/business identity submits, publishes or communicates.
10. Payment is independently verified before revenue is recorded.
11. Settlement Ledger records gross, fees, net, eligibility, receipt and audit events.
12. Learning updates approved performance knowledge and discovery continues.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Pretending GARUDA is a human applicant or legal person
- Self-signing contracts or accepting binding terms without authority
- Claiming unverified earnings
- Bypassing platform terms, age restrictions, identity checks or payment controls
- Spending money or activating paid APIs without approval
- Auto-applying, messaging, publishing or transacting merely because code was approved
- Sacrificing privacy, safety or product integrity for a revenue target
