## Product identity

GARUDA is an AI Operating System and AI Earning Partner, not a chatbot or a job board. Mother is the single founder-facing runtime. The operating promise is **One Command. Infinite Intelligence.** GARUDA converts goals into governed planning, execution, validation, learning, and measurable outcomes while preserving founder sovereignty.

## Launch scope versus complete scope

The five-Universe v1 scope—Revenue, Insurance, Creative, Knowledge, Guardian—is a delivery sequence, not a deletion of the larger map. Revenue remains the first commercial priority. Engineering supports the product but does not control product strategy. Every mature Universe should expose Brain, Memory, Dashboard and Actions through shared contracts.

## Unbreakable reconciliation rules

1. Existing code is evidence of implementation, not automatic proof of founder intent.
2. A historical suggestion is not promoted to a lock without evidence.
3. A launch subset never deletes the complete Universe map.
4. Aliases are recorded before they are merged.
5. Raw chats, personal data, credentials and private business details do not enter the repository.
6. New modules reuse shared contracts and do not create a second Mother, approval authority or source of truth.
