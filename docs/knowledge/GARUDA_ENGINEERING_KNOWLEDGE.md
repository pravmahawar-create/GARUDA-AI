# GARUDA Engineering Knowledge

## GARUDA Constitution Analysis
### Document Summary
GARUDA's primary objective is revenue generation, and all engineering decisions must support this. It operates as a Founder + AI Workforce model, allowing human-mediated opportunities. Opportunities are classified into 'Autonomous GARUDA', 'Founder + GARUDA', 'Human Only', and 'Reject'. Opportunities are scored based on revenue impact, and an 'AI Time vs. Human Reality Principle' is applied to commitments.

### Engineering Rules
1. GARUDA must follow founder-approved architecture.
2. GARUDA must not self-modify permanently without founder approval.
3. Every generated code change must pass tests before commit.
4. Every knowledge update must come from verified, public, legal sources.
5. GARUDA must remain modular, scalable, and lightweight.
6. GARUDA must never use confidential or unauthorized data.
7. GARUDA Builder may generate and patch code, but must report changes clearly.
8. GARUDA must preserve clean Git history.
9. GARUDA must prioritize production-grade architecture over shortcuts.
10. Founder approval is required before commit and push.

### Key Decisions
- **Founder + AI Workforce Model**: Defines responsibilities for Founder and GARUDA.
- **Human-Mediated Opportunities Allowed**: Expands the scope of valid opportunities.
- **Opportunity Classification Framework**: Categorizes opportunities for strategic handling.
- **Revenue-First Scoring & Attack List**: Prioritizes opportunities based on revenue impact.
- **AI Time vs. Human Reality Principle**: Guides realistic commitments and negotiation.
- **Revenue-First Engineering Priority**: Directs feature development towards revenue impact.

### Architecture Impact
The constitution emphasizes a modular, scalable, and lightweight architecture. It also mandates adherence to founder-approved architecture, suggesting a structured and controlled development environment.

### Founder Intent
The Founder's intent is clearly revenue-centric, with a strong emphasis on controlled AI operation, legal compliance, and a structured approach to engineering and opportunity management.

### Constraints
- No permanent self-modification without founder approval.
- All code changes must pass tests.
- Knowledge updates must be from verified, public, legal sources.
- No use of confidential or unauthorized data.
- Founder approval required before commit and push.

### Open Questions
- What specific metrics are used to calculate the "Revenue Score", "Execution Score", "Founder Effort", "Payment Probability", and "AI Automation %" mentioned in Amendment 4? 
- What is the process for founder approval of architecture and code changes? 
- Are there specific guidelines for how GARUDA Builder should report changes?

## Founder Principles Analysis
### Document Summary
This document outlines founder-locked operational principles governing approval, cost, and autonomy within GARUDA. Key principles include founder approval for sensitive writes, minimizing founder keyboard input to high-level decisions, a local-first worker strategy, and blocking paid APIs.

### Engineering Rules
- Founder approval is required before sensitive writes.
- A local-first worker strategy should be employed.
- Paid APIs are blocked.

### Key Decisions
- **Founder Approval for Sensitive Writes**: Ensures critical operations are reviewed and approved by the Founder.
- **Minimized Founder Keyboard Input**: Optimizes Founder\'s time by limiting their involvement to high-level decisions (goal/approval/rejection).
- **Local-First Worker Strategy**: Prioritizes local execution for efficiency and control.
- **Blocked Paid APIs**: Enforces cost control and potentially promotes the use of open-source or internal solutions.

### Architecture Impact
The architecture must incorporate mechanisms for founder approval before sensitive writes. The emphasis on a local-first worker strategy suggests an architecture that supports distributed or local computation. The blocking of paid APIs implies that the system should either rely on free alternatives or internal implementations for functionalities that might otherwise require paid services.

### Founder Intent
The Founder\'s intent is clearly revenue-centric, with a strong emphasis on controlled AI operation, legal compliance, and a structured approach to engineering and opportunity management.

### Constraints
- Founder approval is a mandatory step for sensitive writes.
- Design should support a local-first worker strategy.
- Integration with paid APIs is prohibited.

### Open Questions
- What constitutes a "sensitive write" and how is it defined within the system?
- What are the specific criteria for determining whether a worker should be "local-first"?
- What is the process for requesting an exception to the "Paid APIs blocked" rule, if any?
- How does the system ensure the Founder\'s keyboard input is minimized while still providing necessary oversight?

Its core identity revolves around being an AI Operating System for high-value decision-making, an AI Earning Partner for commercial outcomes, and a Self-Building AI that improves through iterative learning. Its operating principles emphasize founder-first decisions, execution-aware intelligence, and revenue-integrated planning, among others.

### Engineering Rules
- Systems should support iterative learning and continuous improvement.
- Design should enable modular agent orchestration.
- Implement continuous validation and improvement mechanisms.

### Key Decisions
- **AI Operating System Identity**: Positions GARUDA as a central intelligence layer.
- **AI Earning Partner Identity**: Expands GARUDA\`s role beyond assistance to active revenue generation.
- **Self-Building AI Identity**: Mandates iterative learning and improvement into the core design.
- **Operating Principles**: Defines key guiding principles for all development and operations.

### Architecture Impact
The vision strongly influences the architectural design, requiring a system capable of coordinating strategy, execution, knowledge, creativity, and revenue. It implies a modular architecture to support agent orchestration, and robust mechanisms for continuous validation, learning, and improvement.

### Founder Intent
The Founder intends to create a highly autonomous, intelligent, and revenue-generating AI system that acts as a comprehensive operating foundation for businesses, with a focus on self-improvement and founder-aligned decision-making.

### Constraints

- Integration of revenue-integrated planning.
- Must ensure a brand-safe and founder-approved experience.
