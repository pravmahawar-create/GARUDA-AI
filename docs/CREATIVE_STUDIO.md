# Creative Studio

## Overview

The Creative Studio is GARUDA's multimodal content and production intelligence layer. It is structured to support ideation, production, iteration, publishing, and optimization across narrative, visual, and audio experiences.

## Core Capabilities

### Multimodal Creative Engine
The studio supports content generation through text, image, voice, and video inputs. It can compose and refine assets across formats while preserving narrative coherence.

### Text, Image, Voice, and Video Inputs
Creative work can begin from any primary input format and be extended into connected outputs across channels.

### Storyboard Engine
The system can structure visual and narrative sequences into coherent storyboards for campaigns, content launches, and product storytelling.

### Character Consistency
A key requirement for premium content is consistency across visual identity, personality, and representation. GARUDA is designed to preserve that continuity across outputs.

### Timeline Engine
The studio manages sequence, timing, pacing, and production flow for multi-stage creative production.

### Creative Director AI
This intelligence layer evaluates narrative quality, tone, visual alignment, and strategic fit before final publication.

### Publishing Intelligence
Content is optimized for channel fit, audience resonance, and publishing readiness.

### Viral Optimization
The studio incorporates growth logic to increase the probability of broad resonance through format, timing, and narrative refinement.

### Creator Analytics
Performance data is integrated into the creative workflow so output quality can improve with each iteration.

## Creative Workflow

1. Intake concept or raw material
2. Generate and refine multiple directions
3. Validate tone, story, and brand alignment
4. Produce polished creative assets
5. Publish and optimize for performance
