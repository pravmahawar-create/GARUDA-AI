# 05 Brain Standard

## Summary
Defines required brain contract: capability map, policy controls, and reviewability.

## Highlights
- Declared capabilities and action boundaries.
- Approval-required governance.
- Traceable outputs in reports.
- Execution success requires real artifact or command evidence; report-only completion is not success.
- Test execution uses explicit targets, no shell, bounded timeouts, and captured exit evidence.
- Engineering artifacts are generated in an isolated workspace, remain new-file-only in v1, and require Founder approval before source application.
- Intelligence providers are proposal-only adapters: raw provider code is rejected, and allow-listed structured specifications must pass path, isolated execution, and Founder approval gates before they can affect GARUDA source.
- Reviewer Brain independently verifies patch and artifact hashes, real test evidence, and governance flags; its approval is technical only and cannot authorize source, Git, or deployment actions.
- Architect Brain converts bounded goals into dependency-ordered, risk-aware, read-only plans and may hand off only allow-listed structured Engineering specifications.
- Governed correction loops allow at most three attempts, stop on rejection, accept only structured revisions, preserve evidence per attempt, and still require Founder approval.
- Revenue execution missions begin only from Founder-approved, fully verified GARUDA-deliverable candidates with a currently verified commercial capability; bounded scope is required before Engineering begins.
- Founder-approved bounded scopes become dependency-ordered work packages and run only inside the governed Architect → Engineering → Tester → Reviewer evidence loop; completion remains a Founder review checkpoint and never authorizes source, Git, deployment, outreach, contract, spending, payment, or delivery actions.
- Founder mission decisions are immutable, evidence-bound, hash-chained audit records. Approve, request-changes, and reject decisions change mission state but do not authorize any external, financial, source, Git, deployment, or delivery action.
- Request-changes decisions may create a fresh bounded revision with new isolated evidence and Reviewer verdict. Previous scope, evidence, decision, and response hashes remain archived; rejected missions cannot reopen and total evidence revisions are capped at three.
- Founder-approved missions expose a dependency-aware deliverable workspace. Task completion requires evidence, every transition is hash-chained, and workspace progress grants no external-action authority.
- A continuous internal task runner advances only dependency-ready Founder-approved work, retries evidence adapters at most three times, blocks on missing proof, recovers blocked tasks on later cycles, and packages hash-chained run evidence without external-action authority.
- External outreach, application, contract, delivery, deployment, and payment-verification requests each require their own evidence-bound Founder decision. Approval creates a checksum-bound handoff package; completion requires a verifiable receipt, and income is never claimed without payment verification.
- Connector dispatch is allow-listed, action-compatible, idempotent, decision/handoff-hash-bound, and receipt-audited. Sandbox connectors validate the full contract without creating external side effects.
- Revenue acquisition runs continuously on a bounded cadence: it refreshes discovery, rechecks source truth, matches declared capabilities, prepares internal source-grounded drafts, and records blocks. Continuous operation never converts internal preparation into automatic outreach or submission.
- A listing is GARUDA-executable only when it is fresh, source-hash-bound, specific direct client work, and clear of human identity, CV, profile, interview, employment, or personal-experience requirements. Recruitment pages and talent networks are not client missions.
- Proposal approval and submission recording must revalidate the current source snapshot. Changed, missing, or stale source evidence invalidates the pre-submission case and revokes any handoff; it cannot be silently reused.
