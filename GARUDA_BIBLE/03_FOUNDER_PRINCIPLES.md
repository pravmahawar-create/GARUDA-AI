# 03 Founder Principles

## Summary
Founder-locked operational principles for approval, cost, and autonomy.

## Highlights
- Founder approval before sensitive writes.
- Founder keyboard minimized to goal/approval/rejection.
- Local-first worker strategy.
- Paid APIs blocked.

## FD-021 — Every outreach is a mini product experience (PERMANENT)
- Premium copy AND premium, recipient-specific visual presentation are both mandatory parts of a premium GARUDA outreach experience (wherever technically appropriate).
- The recipient should never feel "someone sent me a normal sales email"; they should feel "who are these people — they actually studied my business and prepared something specifically for me."
- Visuals must be specific to the recipient (journey maps, UI/booking/order/dashboard concepts, branded cards, CTA sections); never random stock images, never impersonation, never fabricated brand assets.
- Show the solution: CURRENT JOURNEY → GARUDA CONCEPT → BETTER JOURNEY.
- Medium: email (short + visual teaser + CTA) / personalized GARUDA page (full concept) / proposal (after Founder approval).
- NO fabrication ever; mockups clearly labelled as concepts.
- Founder approval remains required before any external send (verify recipient/opportunity/claims, preview exact email + visuals, then governed send).
