## 1. Part A: Autonomous Revenue Authorization & Low-Risk Policy Model

### Current Mandatory Approval Points
Currently, Founder approval is mandatory at two explicit execution boundaries:
1. **Outbound Communication Dispatch:** `src/services/outboundCommunicationService.js` enforces `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` status. Sending without a Founder approval token throws `HTTP 403 Forbidden`.
2. **Work Authorization:** `src/services/inboundResponseService.js` enforces `WORK_AUTHORIZED` transition before launching governed execution tasks.

### Evidence-Based Policy Model (Target Architecture)
- **LOW-RISK AUTONOMOUS WORK (≤ INR 25,000 equivalent / ~$300 USD):** Can proceed autonomously **if and only if** all 11 safety conditions are met.
- **FOUNDER-APPROVAL WORK (> INR 25,000 equivalent / > $300 USD):** Requires explicit Founder approval token (`x-garuda-founder-approved: true`).

### 11 Safety Conditions for Autonomous Approval (≤ ₹25,000):
1. **Approved Service Category:** Software engineering, technical documentation, or repository quality audit (already exists).
2. **Known/Acceptable Client Context:** Client contact details verified without scam flags (already exists).
3. **Defined Scope:** Well-bounded deliverable requirements without ambiguous terms (already exists).
4. **Known Price:** Clear fixed-price or hourly quote within ₹25,000 threshold (already exists).
5. **Acceptable Risk Score:** Risk score ≤ 25/100 assessed by scam filter (already exists).
6. **Executable Delivery Plan:** Execution task graph generated by Mother Brain (already exists).
7. **Delivery Deadline:** Explicit deadline specified in candidate/contract record (already exists).
8. **Payment Conditions:** Pre-payment or milestone escrow via Razorpay payment link (already exists).
9. **Refund / Dispute Policy:** Standard non-refundable terms for completed technical deliverables (missing explicit policy model).
10. **No Prohibited Activity:** `PROHIBITED_TERMS` and `SCAM_TERMS` clear (already exists).
11. **No Unusual Legal Requirements:** Standard independent contractor terms (missing contract clause evaluator).

## 2. Part B & C: Payment Truth Engine & Provider Verification

### Evidence Disambiguation Matrix
- **CLAIMED PAYMENT:** Text claims ("I paid") map to `PAYMENT_PENDING`. Does **NOT** count as revenue.
- **SCREENSHOT EVIDENCE:** Screenshots treated as **UNVERIFIED**. Does **NOT** count as revenue.
- **PROVIDER-VERIFIED PAYMENT:** Valid Razorpay HMAC SHA-256 signature verified by `paymentWebhookService.js`.
- **SETTLED PAYMENT:** Net payout recorded in `SettlementLedger` (`status: "settled"`). Counts as **REAL REVENUE**.

### P0 Security Rule Check
- **Does any current code treat screenshots or user claims as payment confirmation?** **NO.**
- `paymentWebhookService.js` strictly requires valid HMAC SHA-256 signature matching secret (`crypto.createHmac("sha256", secret)`).
