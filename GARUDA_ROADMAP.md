# GARUDA ROADMAP — REVENUE OPERATING SYSTEM

## Primary Directive
GARUDA exists for ONE PRIMARY OBJECTIVE: **Generate Revenue**.
Roadmap priority is strictly governed by Constitutional Amendment 6: Revenue impact ranks first above all feature decisions.

## Completed & Active Revenue Infrastructure
- **Phase 1: Founder + AI Workforce Intake**: Governed Founder-Assisted Opportunity Intake (`founder_garuda`).
- **Phase 2: Founder Submission Package Engine**: Automated requirement analysis, effort estimation, risk-adjusted pricing recommendation, deliverable planning, and submission packages.
- **Phase 3: Revenue Intelligence Engine (RIE)**: Executive reasoning layer evaluating win/payment probabilities, client quality scoring, negotiation strategy, and AI Time vs. Human Reality economics.
- **Phase 4: Revenue Closing System**: Post-proposal deal closing workflow, Client Conversation Manager, Negotiation Strategy Engine, Proof Before Delivery Engine, Payment Protection Workflow, and Closing Learning Loop.
- **Phase 5: Revenue-First Attack List Engine**: Daily prioritized opportunity attack list ranked by Revenue Score and Expected Profit (`attackListService.js`).
- **Phase 6: GARUDA CRO Intelligence**: Chief Revenue Officer deal winning strategy and automatic outcome learning engine (`garudaCroService.js`).
- **Phase 7: Reality Feedback Engine**: Empirical Deal Tracker & response tracking replacing hardcoded assumptions with real-world outcomes (`dealTrackerService.js`).
- **Phase 8: One-Click Dispatch CLI**: Instant Founder proposal dispatch tool (`npm run garuda:dispatch`).

## Active Milestone: First Paying Client Execution
- **Step 1**: Execute `npm run garuda:dispatch` to generate pre-bundled proposal packages.
- **Step 2**: Founder submits Top 5 proposals to verified live client listings (A.Team, Coalition Tech, Clipster, Lemon.io).
- **Step 3**: Receive Milestone 1 (50%) deposit ($770 – $1,500 USD).
- **Step 4**: Execute technical deliverables & deliver automated test runner logs.
- **Step 5**: Collect final Milestone 2 release.

## Next Infrastructure Milestones
