## 🌟 PART 1: INSTAGRAM KE 3 SECRET BUTTONS (Upload Karte Waqt 1 Minute Ka Kaam)

Jab bhi aap nayi Reel upload karein, "Share" dabane se pehle ye 3 settings zaroor on karein:

### 1. "Add Topics" (Instagram AI Ko Batane Ke Liye)
* Reel upload screen par niche scroll karein ➔ **"Add Topics"** par click karein.
* Ye 3 topics tick karein:
  1. `Babies & Kids`
  2. `Parenting`
  3. `Family & Relationships` (ya `Comedy & Fun`)
* Isse Instagram aapki reel seedhe un logon ke Explore feed me daalta hai jo baby reels dekhte hain.

### 2. "Write Alt Text" (Instagram Search Rank Ke Liye)
* Upload screen par sabse niche **"Advanced Settings"** me jayein.
* Wahan **"Write Alt Text"** par click karein.
* Niche diye gaye templates me se "Alt Text" copy karke wahan paste kar dein.

### 3. Reel Pin Karna (Profile Ka Look Royal Banane Ke Liye)
* Profile par jaakar apni sabse best 3 Reels kholein.
* Top right 3 dots (⋮) par tap karein ➔ **"Pin to your profile"** chunein.
* Isse naya koi bhi account dekhega toh sabse pehle top videos dekhega aur instant Follow karega.
