### 1. What This Agent Does For Your Clinic
This autonomous AI agent acts as your 24/7 Digital Patient Care Coordinator on your website:
* **Immediate Triage**: Warmly greets visitors, answers treatment FAQs, and immediately distinguishes between routine appointments and urgent pain/swelling emergencies.
* **Transparent Pricing & Service Guidance**: Accurately explains and guides patients across 5 core procedures (Invisalign Clear Aligners, Laser Whitening, Single & Full-Mouth Implants, Emergency Relief, Routine Cleaning).
* **Patient Intake & Lead Capture**: Gathers the patient's full name, phone number, and preferred appointment time.
* **Emergency Safety Guardrail**: If trauma, severe unmanageable pain, or acute swelling is reported, the agent flags it as a priority emergency and provides immediate clinic walk-in instructions.

---

### 2. What To Check During Weekly Review
To ensure optimal patient conversion:
1. **Intake Accuracy**: Verify that captured patient names, callback numbers, and preferred appointment windows align with front-desk calendar slots.
2. **Pricing Consistency**: Review monthly package updates (e.g. promotional offers on whitening or aligners) against the agent's knowledge base.
3. **Escalation Logs**: Check how many emergency triage interactions occurred outside regular clinic hours.

---

### 3. How To Update & Refine The Agent
Updating clinic hours, procedure pricing, or doctor availability takes less than 2 minutes on Agentplace:
1. **Open Workspace**: Navigate to your Agentplace Dashboard (`agentplace.io/admin/dashboard`).
2. **Edit Instructions**: Click on **Builder** and enter your update (e.g., *"Update routine cleaning fee from $89 to $99"* or *"Add Saturday 10 AM - 2 PM operating hours"*).
3. **Preview & Verify**: Test the updated flow in the right-hand **Preview** window.
4. **Publish**: Click the black **Publish** button in the top right to instantly push changes live to `https://tspebf1ycyxc.run.agentplace.io/`.
