* **Minute 00–03 | The Hemorrhage (Problem Framing)**:
  Highlight the industry reality: 35% of high-intent dental inquiries happen after 5:00 PM or on weekends. Traditional voicemail leads to patient drop-off (average loss: $1,500 - $3,500 per lost implant/aligner inquiry).
* **Minute 03–10 | Live Interactive Walkthrough**:
  * Open `https://tspebf1ycyxc.run.agentplace.io/` on screen.

  * Trigger **High-Ticket Exploration**: Show how a patient asks about Clear Aligners ($3,500 package) and receives instant transparent pricing breakdown without hesitation.
  * Demonstrate **Voice ("Speak") Interaction**: Let the practice manager speak directly to the agent to hear sub-second natural voice response.

  Show how patient name, phone number, and preferred timeslot are captured cleanly and mapped to front-desk notification workflows.
* **Minute 15–20 | Q&A, Technical Objection Handling & Next Steps**.

### 3. Top 3 Expected Objections & How to Neutralize Them

#### Objection 1: "Will the AI give inaccurate clinical advice or diagnose patients?"
* **Answer**: *"Absolutely not. The agent is strictly locked with clinical safety guardrails. It never diagnoses. It triages urgency (routine vs. acute emergency), provides transparent administrative pricing, and gathers intake details for doctor review."*

#### Objection 2: "What if our clinic hours or procedure fees change next month?"
* **Answer**: *"Updating takes 60 seconds on Agentplace Builder. Any non-technical clinic coordinator can simply type 'Update aligner price to $3,200' in plain English, click Publish, and the live web agent updates instantly with zero code."*

#### Objection 3: "Does this replace our front-desk staff?"
* **Answer**: *"No. It empowers them. It filters out repetitive after-hours FAQs and delivers pre-qualified patient appointment requests directly to their inbox every morning so staff focus purely on in-clinic patient care."*
